# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Architecture Atlas is a learning community. We want everyone, from first-time contributors to
senior architects, to be able to ask questions, propose corrections, and disagree about trade-offs
without fear of ridicule or harassment.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and patient. Many readers are learning this material for the first time.
- Critique ideas, designs, and claims, not people. "This diagram omits the failure path" is
  useful; "this is stupid" is not.
- Back technical disagreements with sources and reasoning, and accept that reasonable architects
  often disagree about trade-offs.
- Credit other people's work and respect licensing rules.
- Assume good intent, and ask for clarification before escalating.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or demeaning language of any kind.
- Publishing others' private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, reviews, or issues.
- Plagiarism or knowingly submitting content you do not have the right to license.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces: the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, and any
official communication channel, and to anyone representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to the maintainers through a private GitHub security advisory
(<https://github.com/vinay9354/architecture-atlas/security/advisories/new>) marked "Conduct", or
by contacting a maintainer directly through GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially.
Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions, comments, or issues that violate this
code, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation for repeated or severe violations.
