# Data engineering, analytics, and big data

Operational databases answer "what is the state now?"; analytical platforms answer "what happened,
why, and what next?" This section covers data modeling and governance, batch and streaming pipelines,
the warehouse/lake/lakehouse/mesh landscape, the big-data engines and open table formats behind them,
and dimensional modeling for business intelligence.

## What you will learn here

- Build conceptual, logical, and physical data models with lineage and ownership
- Design ETL/ELT and CDC pipelines with validation, schema evolution, and reconciliation
- Choose between warehouse, lake, lakehouse, and mesh for an organization's needs
- Explain how Spark, Flink, and open table formats process and store data at scale
- Design star schemas and slowly changing dimensions for analytics

**Prerequisites:** [Databases, storage, and caching](../databases-and-storage/index.md) and [Messaging and streaming](../messaging-and-streaming/index.md).
