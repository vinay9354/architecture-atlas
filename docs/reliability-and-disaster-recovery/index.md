# High availability, reliability, resilience, and disaster recovery

Availability is engineered, not hoped for. This section covers redundancy and fault domains,
availability arithmetic, failover and health checks, reliability engineering methods (FMEA, fault
trees, chaos engineering), disaster-recovery strategies from backup/restore to active-active, and
advanced topics such as blast radius, cascading failure, and split-brain prevention.

## What you will learn here

- Calculate availability for serial and parallel components and set realistic targets
- Eliminate single points of failure across zones and regions
- Choose a DR strategy from RTO/RPO targets and cost, and test it
- Contain failures with cells, bulkheads, and degraded modes

**Prerequisites:** [Quality attributes and trade-offs](../architecture-fundamentals/quality-attributes-and-trade-offs.md) and [Distributed systems](../distributed-systems/index.md).
