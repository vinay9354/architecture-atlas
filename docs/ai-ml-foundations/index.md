# AI and machine learning foundations

Before designing AI systems you need to know what a model is, how it is trained, how it fails, and
what the lifecycle around it looks like. This section covers AI/ML/deep-learning distinctions and
learning paradigms, classical machine learning, the mathematics that matters (vectors, similarity,
gradients, probability), neural networks and transformers, the ML lifecycle, MLOps, and ML
infrastructure. The mathematics is explained from first principles for readers without a math degree.

## What you will learn here

- Distinguish AI, ML, deep learning, and generative AI, and the learning paradigms
- Explain classical algorithms and evaluation, overfitting, and bias-variance
- Use vectors, dot products, cosine similarity, and gradient descent intuitively
- Describe how neural networks and transformers work and why attention matters
- Design an ML lifecycle with registries, monitoring, drift detection, and retraining

**Prerequisites:** [Foundations](../foundations/index.md); no prior ML knowledge assumed.
