# Enterprise architecture, governance, hybrid cloud, and modernization

Enterprise architecture connects business strategy to technology portfolios across many systems and
years. This section covers the practice (capability maps, portfolios, principles, review boards), the
frameworks (TOGAF, Zachman, ArchiMate, ITIL, COBIT, FEAF), legacy modernization (the 7 Rs, strangler
patterns, migration waves), and hybrid/multi-cloud strategy including exit and lock-in considerations.

## What you will learn here

- Build a capability map and application portfolio and use them for decisions
- Explain the purpose, scope, strengths, and limits of the major frameworks
- Plan a modernization or migration program with waves, cutover, and rollback
- Design hybrid and multi-cloud identity, network, data, and governance

**Prerequisites:** [Architecture fundamentals](../architecture-fundamentals/index.md) and [Cloud architecture](../cloud-architecture/index.md).
