# Advanced and emerging architectures

This section covers architecture areas that are either advanced or still settling: edge and
distributed cloud, real-time systems, search architecture, blockchain and Web3, privacy-preserving
computing (confidential computing, homomorphic encryption, federated learning), cyber resilience, and
advanced AI directions (reasoning models, mixture of experts, autonomous software engineering,
self-healing systems). Pages here are dated more carefully because the ground moves.

## What you will learn here

- Design for intermittent connectivity and edge placement
- Explain inverted indexes, relevance, and hybrid search
- Evaluate privacy-preserving techniques for a data-sharing problem
- Separate durable ideas from hype in emerging AI architecture

**Prerequisites:** Varies by page; each page lists its own.
