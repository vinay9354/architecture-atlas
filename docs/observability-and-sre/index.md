# Observability, monitoring, SRE, and production operations

You cannot operate what you cannot see. This section covers logs, metrics, traces, and profiles;
OpenTelemetry and telemetry pipelines; monitoring, golden signals, alerting, and on-call; and the
operational practices (incident management, postmortems, runbooks, production readiness, SLO
management) that Site Reliability Engineering (SRE) made mainstream.

## What you will learn here

- Instrument a service with correlated logs, metrics, and traces
- Design dashboards and alerts around golden signals and SLOs rather than noise
- Run incidents and blameless postmortems
- Define production-readiness and service ownership for a platform

**Prerequisites:** [Reliability and disaster recovery](../reliability-and-disaster-recovery/index.md).
