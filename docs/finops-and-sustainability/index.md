# FinOps, cost architecture, and sustainability

Cost is an architecture quality attribute. This section covers cloud pricing models and commitment
discounts, cost allocation and unit economics (cost per transaction, per customer, per AI inference),
TCO and ROI, and sustainable, carbon-aware architecture. The aim is to make cost visible at design time
rather than at invoice time.

## What you will learn here

- Explain pay-as-you-go, reserved, savings plans, and spot, and when each fits
- Tag, allocate, and forecast cost, and compute unit economics
- Identify the design decisions that drive cost (egress, over-provisioning, idle, data lifecycle)
- Reduce energy and carbon through right-sizing, scheduling, and region selection

**Prerequisites:** [Cloud architecture](../cloud-architecture/index.md).
