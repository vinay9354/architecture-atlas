## Why two licenses?

Code and prose have different reuse patterns. MIT is the conventional, permissive choice for
code; CC BY 4.0 is designed for text and media and requires attribution, which keeps the
learning material traceable to its source. The decision and its trade-offs are recorded in
[ADR-0002](docs/appendices/adr/adr-0002-license-strategy.md).
